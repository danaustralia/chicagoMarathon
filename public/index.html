09 / HOW TO READ THE DATA

Aggregate insights.
No individual profiles.

Race results are processed locally. Only summary counts, distributions and reference values are used by this public dashboard.

Definitions and limitations

Pacing: negative and positive splits are classified using a ±2% even-split band. Late slowdown compares pace at 30–40K with 10–20K, using runners with valid checkpoints.

Age: age-group charts use recorded Chicago divisions; the same runner appears only in their outcome category. Missing divisions are not assigned an estimated age.

Geography: CTZ suffixes in result names are source country codes, not verified nationality or residence.

Weather: observations were supplied for this analysis and were not independently verified. Weather and pacing correlations do not establish causation.

Boston: meeting a reference qualifying standard does not guarantee entry; race-day age is approximated from Chicago divisions.

Privacy: no names, bibs, individual split histories or runner identifiers belong in the public JSON. Keep the source SQLite database and private exports off Wasmer.
